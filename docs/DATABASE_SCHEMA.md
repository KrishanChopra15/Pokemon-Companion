# Database Schema

This document outlines the primary MongoDB collections used by Pokémon Companion.

---

## Users

Stores trainer accounts, authentication information, settings, and personal progress.

**Status:** Planned

---

## Pokemon

Stores complete Pokémon data, including stats, types, abilities, forms, evolutions, and Pokédex information.

**Status:** Planned

---

## Moves

Stores every Pokémon move, including power, accuracy, PP, type, category, effects, and learning methods.

**Status:** Planned

---

## Abilities

Stores all Pokémon abilities, including descriptions, battle effects, and Pokémon that can learn them.

**Status:** Planned

---

## Items

Stores held items, healing items, evolution items, berries, TMs, key items, and battle items.

**Status:** Planned

---

## Games

Stores every supported Pokémon game, including generation, release information, regional Pokédex, and exclusive content.

**Status:** Planned

---

## Regions

Stores information about every Pokémon region, including towns, routes, landmarks, and map data.

**Status:** Planned

---

## Routes

Stores route-specific information such as available Pokémon, trainers, items, and encounters.

**Status:** Planned

---

## Cities

Stores towns and cities, including Gyms, Pokémon Centers, Poké Marts, NPCs, and important locations.

**Status:** Planned

---

## Achievements

Stores achievement definitions, milestones, badges, and completion rewards.

**Status:** Planned

---

## Collections

Stores each trainer's personal collection, including owned Pokémon, shiny Pokémon, forms, and Living Dex progress.

**Status:** Planned

---

## Tracker

Stores game progression, completed Gyms, Elite Four status, quests, story milestones, and adventure history.

**Status:** Planned

---

## Friends

Stores trainer friendships, friend requests, comparisons, and shared progress.

**Status:** Planned

---
